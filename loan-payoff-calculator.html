  • You'll see how much interest you save and how many years the loan shortens.

Why extra payments work

Extra payments go straight to your principal. That reduces the balance the interest is calculated on, so you pay less interest and finish the loan sooner.

On a 30-year mortgage, even a modest extra payment each month can cut years off your term and save tens of thousands of dollars — which is why so many homeowners accelerate.

Common questions

Is it worth paying off my loan early?▾
Paying extra cuts total interest and shortens the loan, but only makes sense if your extra cash could earn more elsewhere or if you have other high-interest debt. Compare the numbers here first.
How much does one extra payment a year save?▾
On a typical 30-year mortgage, making one extra payment a year can cut the term by several years and save tens of thousands in interest, depending on the rate.
What's the difference between biweekly and extra payments?▾
Biweekly payments split your payment in half every two weeks, which equals 13 full payments a year — effectively one extra payment annually. Both strategies reduce interest and shorten the term.
