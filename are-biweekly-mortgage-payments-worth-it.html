Are Biweekly Mortgage Payments Worth It?

A simple schedule trick that can knock years off your mortgage.

Related: Payoff Calculator · Is Early Payoff Worth It
The short answer
Yes — if you can do it free. It's really one extra payment a year.

Biweekly payments add up to 13 full payments a year, which shortens the loan and saves interest. But skip plans that charge a fee — you can do the same thing yourself for free.

How biweekly payments work

Instead of 12 monthly payments, you pay half your monthly payment every two weeks. Because there are 52 weeks in a year, that's 26 half-payments — which equals 13 full monthly payments, or one extra payment a year.

That extra payment goes straight to principal. It reduces the balance your interest is calculated on, so you pay less interest and finish the loan sooner.

What it saves you

  • Shorter term — on a 30-year loan, biweekly payments can cut the term by several years.
  • Lower total interest — typically saving tens of thousands of dollars.
  • Built-in discipline — the extra payment happens automatically.

See the exact numbers with our Payoff Calculator — enter the extra amount you'd pay each year.

Watch the catch

Many banks charge a fee for biweekly programs. Some even delay your first payment, which cancels out the benefit. Check your servicer's terms — or just make one extra payment a year yourself, which is free and works the same.

If your budget allows, the cleanest approach is an extra monthly payment or an annual lump sum — no fee, same interest savings.
