Which debt should you pay off first?

If you have multiple loans, here's the optimal order based on math:

  • 1. High-interest credit cards first (15-25% APR). They cost more than any loan. Pay these off before touching anything else.
  • 2. Personal loans and car loans (5-15% APR). These are next — they're not tax-deductible like mortgage interest.
  • 3. Mortgage last (3-7% APR). Mortgage interest is tax-deductible, and you can often invest your extra cash for a higher return than the interest you save.

Exception: If you hate debt more than you like money, pay off everything in any order — psychological peace of mind has value too.

This is general educational information, not financial advice. Interest savings depend on your rate, term, and how long you stay. Confirm details with your lender.
