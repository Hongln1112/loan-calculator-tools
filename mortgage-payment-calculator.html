How to use this calculator

  • Enter the loan amount you plan to borrow, the annual interest rate, and the term in years.
  • Tap Calculate payment to see your monthly payment, total interest, and total amount paid over the loan.
  • Try a shorter term (like 15 years) to see how much interest you save versus a 30-year loan.

What's included in your payment

This shows principal and interest only. Your real monthly cost often also includes property taxes, homeowners insurance, and possibly PMI or HOA fees — ask your lender for the full escrow amount.

The formula used here is the standard amortization formula: your monthly rate is the annual rate divided by 12, and the payment is spread across the full number of payments so that the loan is fully paid off at the end of the term.

Common questions

How is my monthly mortgage payment calculated?▾
Your monthly payment is calculated from the loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan term. The annual rate is divided by 12 for a monthly rate, then principal and interest are spread across the number of payments.
What does my monthly payment include?▾
This calculator shows just principal and interest. Your real payment often also includes property taxes, homeowners insurance, and sometimes PMI or HOA fees.
How much interest will I pay over the life of the loan?▾
Over a full 30-year term, interest can easily equal or exceed the amount you borrowed. Use the total interest figure here to see it, then try a shorter term to see how much you save.
Should I choose a 15-year or 30-year mortgage?▾
A 15-year loan has a higher monthly payment but far less total interest. A 30-year loan has a lower monthly payment but much more interest over time. Compare both here before you choose.
