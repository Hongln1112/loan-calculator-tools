How to use this calculator

  • Enter the principal (amount borrowed), the annual rate, and the term.
  • Tap Calculate interest to see your monthly payment, the total interest, and the full cost of the loan.
  • This works for car loans, personal loans, and any amortizing loan — not just mortgages.

Why interest adds up so fast

Interest compounds on the remaining balance each month. Early payments are mostly interest, so the longer the term, the more you pay overall — even at the same rate.

Try comparing a 5-year and a 10-year term at the same rate here. You'll see the shorter term costs far less in total interest, even though the monthly payment is higher.

Common questions

How do I calculate the total interest on a loan?▾
Multiply your monthly payment by the number of payments, then subtract the principal. That difference is the total interest. This calculator does exactly that for you.
Why do I pay so much more than I borrowed?▾
Because interest accrues on the remaining balance each month. On a 30-year loan, the interest over time can approach or exceed the amount you borrowed. A shorter term dramatically reduces it.
How can I pay less interest?▾
Lower the rate, shorten the term, or make extra principal payments. Even a small extra payment each month can save thousands in interest over the life of the loan.
