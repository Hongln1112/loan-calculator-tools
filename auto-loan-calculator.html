How to use this calculator

  • Enter the car price, your down payment, the annual interest rate, and the term in months.
  • Tap Calculate payment to see your loan amount, monthly payment, total interest, and the full cost of the car.
  • Your down payment comes off the price, so a bigger down payment means a smaller loan and less interest.

Get the best rate

Shop before you go to the dealer. Banks and credit unions often beat dealer financing. Getting pre-approved gives you leverage to negotiate the price and the rate separately.

Your credit score is the biggest factor in your rate. Paying off credit cards and checking your report for errors before you apply can save you a lot over the loan.

Common questions

How do I calculate my car loan payment?▾
Subtract your down payment and trade-in from the car price to get the loan amount, then apply the interest rate and term. This calculator does it for you.
What's a good interest rate for a car loan?▾
Rates depend on your credit and the market. A shorter term and higher credit score usually get you a lower rate. Compare quotes from banks, credit unions, and the dealership.
Should I use a shorter or longer car loan term?▾
A shorter term (like 36 or 48 months) means a higher payment but far less interest. Longer terms lower the payment but cost much more overall. Choose based on what fits your budget.
