How to use this calculator

  • Enter the home price, your monthly rent, the mortgage rate, your down payment %, and how long you plan to stay.
  • Tap Compare to see your mortgage payment, how much interest you pay in that time, what you'd spend on rent, and how much equity you build.
  • The longer you stay, the more equity you build — which is the heart of the buy-vs-rent decision.

What this comparison misses

This is a simplified view. Buying also brings property taxes, insurance, maintenance, and closing costs. Renting avoids repairs but has no equity. Factor those in before deciding.

Homeownership also has non-financial benefits — stability, control, and the ability to build wealth. Renting offers flexibility to move. There's no single right answer; it depends on your plans.

Common questions

Is it better to buy or rent?▾
Buying builds equity and can pay off over time, but ties up cash and has large upfront costs. Renting is flexible with lower upfront costs but builds no equity. The right answer depends on how long you'll stay and your local market.
How long should I stay before buying makes sense?▾
A common rule of thumb is 5 to 7 years. The closing costs, down payment, and ongoing maintenance need time to be recovered through equity and appreciation before buying beats renting.
What costs does renting avoid?▾
Renting avoids the down payment, closing costs, property taxes, insurance, maintenance, and repairs that homeownership brings. Your rent may rise over time, but you have no repair bills.
