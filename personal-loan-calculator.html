  • Enter the loan amount, the annual interest rate, and the term in years.

  • This works for debt consolidation, home improvement, or any unsecured personal loan.

Watch the rate

A few points can cost thousands. Compare the annual percentage rate (APR), not just the interest rate — APR includes fees. On a longer term, a small rate difference adds up quickly.

Your credit score drives your rate. Before applying, pay down credit cards and shop at least a few lenders. A pre-qualification check lets you see rates without hurting your score.

Common questions

How is my personal loan payment calculated?▾
Personal loans are usually amortized: your monthly payment is fixed, with part going to interest and part to principal, until the loan is paid off at the end of the term. This calculator uses that standard formula.
What factors affect my personal loan rate?▾
Your credit score is the biggest factor, along with the loan amount, term, and lender. Comparing offers from several lenders is the best way to find a lower rate.
Is a personal loan cheaper than a credit card?▾
Personal loans usually have much lower interest rates than credit cards, especially for consolidating debt. But terms vary, so compare the actual rates and fees before choosing.
