How to use this calculator

  • Enter the monthly payment you can comfortably afford, the annual rate, and the term.
  • Tap Calculate to see the total loan amount that fits that monthly payment, plus the interest you'd pay over the term.
  • Lower the rate or lengthen the term and the amount you can borrow goes up — but so does the interest.

What lenders consider

A common guideline: keep your monthly housing payment under about 28% of gross income, and total debts under about 36%. This calculator shows the loan that matches a payment you pick — your lender also checks income, credit and debt.

Remember your monthly payment usually also includes property taxes and insurance. If you plan to spend $1,800 on the full payment, budget a little less for principal and interest to leave room.

Common questions

How much house can I afford?▾
A common guideline is that your monthly housing payment should not exceed about 28% of your gross monthly income, and total debts should stay under about 36%. This calculator works backward from a monthly payment you're comfortable with.
What does my monthly payment need to cover?▾
Your payment covers principal and interest, and often also property taxes, insurance, and sometimes HOA or PMI. This calculator estimates principal and interest; leave room for the rest.
How much do I need for a down payment?▾
Common down payments range from 3% to 20%. Higher down payments mean a smaller loan and lower monthly payment. Your lender can tell you what your loan program requires.
