About Loan Calculator Tools

We build free, browser-based calculators and clear guides to help you understand your borrowing numbers before you sign anything.

What we do

Loan Calculator Tools is a collection of free loan and mortgage calculators plus practical guides. Our goal is simple: help you see the real cost of borrowing, so you can make decisions with confidence.

  • Calculate monthly payments, total interest, and full loan cost.
  • Work backward from a budget to see how much you can borrow.
  • Compare buying vs renting, and fixed vs adjustable mortgages.
  • See how extra payments or refinancing can save you interest over time.

How it works

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter never leave your device — we don't store them, transmit them, or ask you to create an account. Just enter your numbers, see the result, and go.

Why we built this

Borrowing money is one of the biggest financial decisions most people make. Yet the math behind mortgages, car loans, and personal loans is often hidden behind fine print and lender jargon. We believe the numbers should be easy to see, easy to understand, and available to everyone for free.

Important disclaimer

All calculators on this site provide estimates for general guidance only and are not financial advice. Loan terms, rates, and fees vary by lender. Always confirm details with a qualified mortgage professional or financial advisor before making borrowing decisions.
