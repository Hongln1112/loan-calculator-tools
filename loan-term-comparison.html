Which should you choose?

The 30-year isn't automatically worse. The lower payment frees cash you could invest. If your investment return beats your mortgage rate, the 30-year can come out ahead despite paying more interest.

But if you want guaranteed savings and to own your home debt-free sooner, the 15-year is hard to beat — often saving more than half the total interest.

Common questions

Is a 15-year mortgage worth it?▾
A 15-year mortgage has a higher monthly payment but far less total interest — often saving tens of thousands or more. It's worth it if you can afford the higher payment and want to own your home sooner.
How much does a 30-year mortgage cost vs a 15-year?▾
A 30-year loan has a lower monthly payment but roughly doubles the total interest compared to a 15-year at the same rate. The shorter term also usually comes with a lower rate.
Which mortgage term should I choose?▾
Choose a 15-year if you can comfortably afford the payment and want to minimize interest. Choose a 30-year if you need a lower payment or prefer to invest the difference. Consider your cash flow and goals.
