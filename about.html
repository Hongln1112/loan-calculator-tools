About Loan Calculator Tools

What we do

Loan Calculator Tools is a free collection of loan and mortgage calculators plus practical guides. Our goal is simple: help you understand your borrowing numbers before you sign anything.

  • Calculate monthly payments, total interest, and full loan cost.
  • See how much you can borrow from a payment that fits your budget.
  • Compare buying vs renting, and fixed vs adjustable mortgages.
  • Understand rates, down payments, and whether prepaying is worth it.

Our approach

Everything on this site is free and runs entirely in your browser. We don't collect your financial details, we don't require sign-up, and we don't sell data. The calculators use standard financial formulas to give you accurate estimates.

How our numbers are calculated

Every calculator on this site uses the standard amortization formula that lenders themselves use, based on widely accepted financial practice:

  • Monthly payments — computed from the loan amount, annual interest rate, and term, using the equal-amortization payment formula (principal + interest spread evenly across the term).
  • Affordability — works backward from a monthly payment you choose to the loan amount it supports, using the present-value formula.
  • Total interest & total paid — derived directly from those payment figures over the full term.
  • Guidelines — affordability and down-payment guidance reference common lending standards such as the 28/36 debt-to-income guideline, which is a widely used benchmark in U.S. mortgage lending.

We aim to keep figures transparent and consistent so you can reproduce any result yourself.

Editorial & update process

We take accuracy seriously, especially on a site about money. Our process is simple but deliberate:

  • Guides are written to reflect common U.S. lending practices and consumer guidance (for example, concepts promoted by agencies like the CFPB and HUD), and are reviewed before publishing.
  • We refresh guides and calculators on an ongoing basis as market conditions and practices change.
  • Where numbers depend on your individual situation — your credit, your lender, your local market — we say so clearly and steer you to confirm the details with a qualified professional.

Why we can be trusted

  • Free, private, transparent. No sign-up, no data collection, no hidden fees — just the math, shown clearly.
  • No sales agenda. We don't push any lender, product, or broker. The calculators are genuinely neutral.
  • Clear boundaries. We always label estimates as estimates, and we're explicit about what's general education vs personal financial advice.
  • Plain-language honesty. If something depends on your lender or market, we tell you instead of pretending one number fits everyone.

A note on accuracy

Our calculators provide estimates using standard formulas. Real loan terms depend on your lender, credit, fees, and local market. Please treat these numbers as guidance and confirm with a qualified professional before making a financial decision.
