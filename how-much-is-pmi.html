How Much Is PMI?

PMI is the price you pay for a small down payment — here's what it actually costs and how to get rid of it.

Related: Down Payment Guide · Mortgage Payment
The short answer
PMI runs about 0.5% to 1.5% of the loan amount per year.

On a $300,000 loan, that's roughly $125 to $375 a month. Your credit score and down payment set your exact rate.

PMI cost at a glance

Loan amount0.5% / yr1.0% / yr1.5% / yr
$200,000$83 / mo$167 / mo$250 / mo
$300,000$125 / mo$250 / mo$375 / mo
$400,000$167 / mo$333 / mo$500 / mo

Smaller down payments and lower credit scores push your PMI rate toward the higher end.

When PMI disappears

PMI isn't permanent. On a conventional loan, you can request removal once your equity reaches 20%, and lenders must remove it automatically at 22% — as long as you're current on payments.

You can also reach 20% faster with extra payments. Check our Payoff Calculator to see how quickly you'd get there.

How to avoid PMI entirely

  • Put down 20% — the simplest way.
  • Choose a no-PMI loan — some programs work with less down.
  • Lender-paid mortgage insurance — a higher rate instead of PMI.
  • VA or USDA loans — often no PMI at all.

Each option has trade-offs, so compare the full cost before choosing.

Common questions

How much does PMI cost?▾
PMI typically costs 0.5% to 1.5% of the original loan amount per year. On a $300,000 loan that's roughly $125 to $375 a month. Your credit score and down payment set your exact rate.
When can I cancel PMI?▾
On a conventional loan, you can request removal once your equity reaches 20% of the original value, and it must be removed automatically at 22% — as long as you're current on payments.
How do I avoid PMI?▾
Put down 20%, choose a loan type without PMI, or ask about lender-paid mortgage insurance. Some programs allow lower down payments with no PMI.
Is PMI tax deductible?▾
The deductibility of PMI has changed over time. Check the current tax rules for the year, as it has been allowed in some years and limited or expired in others.
