Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Overview

We believe your financial details are your business. Loan Calculator Tools is built to work entirely in your browser, so we don't collect the numbers you enter into our calculators. This policy explains what data we do (and don't) handle, how third-party services such as advertising networks may use cookies, and your rights under applicable privacy laws.

What we collect

  • Calculation inputs: never stored, transmitted, or logged. They stay on your device and are processed entirely by your browser.
  • Personal information: we don't ask for or collect any — no accounts, no sign-ups, no email capture.
  • Server logs: like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps) for security and performance. We do not use these to identify you individually.
  • Our own cookies: this site does not set its own tracking cookies.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. These cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and to measure ad performance.

Specifically:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites across the Internet.
  • DoubleClick DART cookie may be used to serve personalized ads based on your interests.

How to opt out: You can opt out of Google's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. Note that opting out does not stop ads entirely — you may still see contextual (non-personalized) ads.

GDPR and CCPA

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have certain data protection rights. Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to opt out of the sale of personal information, and to request deletion.

Since this site does not collect personal data directly, these rights largely apply to data collected by our third-party advertising partners. You can manage your ad preferences directly through Google's Ads Settings linked above.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data retention

Since we do not collect personal data from visitors, there is no personal data for us to retain. Standard server logs are kept by our hosting provider for up to 30 days for security and performance purposes, after which they are automatically deleted.
