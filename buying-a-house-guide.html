Common first-time buyer mistakes

  • 1. Buying more house than you need. Lenders pre-approve you based on your income, not your comfort. A $400k house feels cheap when you're used to paying $1,800 rent — until you add property tax, insurance, and maintenance. Aim for 25% less than your pre-approval.
  • 2. Forgetting closing costs. Closing costs are 2-5% of the purchase price — not just the down payment. On a $300k house, that's $6k-$15k extra. Budget for it.
  • 3. Skipping the home inspection. It's $300-$500, but it saves you from buying a house with hidden problems. Never waive your inspection unless you're buying as-is at a deep discount.
  • 4. Not shopping around for a mortgage. The first lender you call isn't the best deal. Get quotes from 3-5 lenders — a 0.25% rate difference saves you tens of thousands over 30 years.
